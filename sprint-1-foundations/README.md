# Sprint 1 — AI Foundations & Career Strategy

**Tools:** ChatGPT · Prompt Engineering · AI-Assisted Documentation
**Sprint:** 1 of 7 | TripleTen AI Automation Bootcamp

---

## What This Sprint Covered

Sprint 1 established the foundation — both technical and professional — for the rest of the bootcamp.

Technical focus:
- Working with large language models through structured prompting
- Understanding how prompt engineering affects output quality and reliability
- Using AI tools for documentation, analysis, and professional communication

Career focus:
- Self-assessment of transferable skills across prior roles
- Job search strategy development for the AI Automation Specialist role
- Positioning non-traditional background (operations, compliance, music production) for a tech-adjacent career pivot

---

## Deliverables

- Self-Reflection Reports (Exercises 1-3) — skills inventory, career positioning, and gap analysis
- Job Search Strategy document — target roles, target industries, positioning approach
- AI communication exercises — structured prompting practice across multiple use cases

---

## Key Principle Established Here

AI tools are not a shortcut — they are an amplifier. The quality of the output is a direct function of the quality of the input. That principle runs through every sprint that follows.

---

## Background That Made This Different

10+ years of operational leadership, compliance documentation, and music production before this program. The transferable skills — building systems, writing clear procedures, making sure nothing falls through the cracks — applied directly to automation work from sprint one.
